Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

Core Philosophy: Your data is yours. It stays on your device. We do not host any remote tracking servers, and we never collect, upload, or sell your financial histories.

Welcome to KharchaDekh. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details how the KharchaDekh mobile application handles your data locally on your device.

1. Zero Server-Side Transmission

KharchaDekh is built to run entirely offline:

  • No Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or link any external credentials to use the app.
  • No Analytics/Tracking: We do not employ third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics tools.
  • No Internet Needed: The core features of the app function completely offline.

2. Notification Listener Access

With your explicit consent, KharchaDekh utilizes Android's Notification Listener Service. This runs locally on your phone to parse financial SMS notifications sent by banking and UPI applications (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.):

  • Local Parsing: The notification texts are processed strictly on-device to extract transaction amounts, credit/debit indicators, and merchant names.
  • Strict Filtering: The app actively ignores all non-financial alerts. Only specific transaction messages are processed.
  • Local DB Storage: Categorized ledger logs are saved securely inside the app's local sandbox SQLite database.

3. Local and Cloud Backups (SAF)

KharchaDekh lets you back up and restore your local SQLite ledger database files. This features uses Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF):

  • We do not operate any database sync servers.
  • When you perform a backup, you choose where to save the file (local folder, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox) synced on your Android device.
  • Your data is written directly to your own storage container, keeping your data entirely under your personal control.

4. DPDP Act 2023 & User Control

In full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, we provide complete, transparent control over your local data:

  • Right to Erase: You can erase all transaction entries, custom categories, and consent states instantly using the "Flush All Ledger States" button in the Settings panel.
  • Explicit Consent: You have full control over permission grants. If you decline Notification Access, you can still use the app manually.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Since we do not collect user email addresses or phone numbers, we recommend checking this page periodically. Any modifications will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top.

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding local privacy practices, you can contact the developer at: support@ankitsudegora.com
