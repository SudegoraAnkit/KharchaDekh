Namaste! Welcome to KharchaDekh, India's privacy-first automated expense tracker.

Initial Release Features:
- 100% Local & Offline: Notification parsing runs strictly on-device (compliant with the DPDP Act 2023).
- Auto-Tracking: Spends/credits parsed automatically from UPI & banking app notifications.
- Budgets & Categories: Customise categories and monthly spending limits.
- Cash Logging: Quick manual entries for offline cash transactions.
- Cloud Backup: Sync databases securely to Google Drive or OneDrive using Android SAF.
- Reports: Export transaction ledger to formatted PDF or Excel CSV files.
- Reminders: Scheduled daily check-ins to stay on budget.
